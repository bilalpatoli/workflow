01 Why we built this

Training does not scale when knowledge is trapped in docs.

We have built offshore teams for our own companies and for clients — including 100+ team members in the Philippines for our marketing agency, a 6-person AI team, and 800+ hires across 112 enterprise clients through our BPO.

The same problem kept showing up.

  • Managers record Looms.
  • Teams create SOPs.
  • Checklists get shared.
  • Slack threads pile up.
  • New hires still miss steps.

The issue is not a lack of documentation. The issue is that documentation does not automatically create execution.

02 The execution loop

Train. Test. Evaluate. Retrain.

Most training tools stop at content. WorkFlow runs the full loop — the same loop a manager runs manually every day. We wanted to see how much of it an agent could automate.

03 The hypothetical demo scenario

Training offshore cold callers on a complex sales SOP.

An offshore team needs to be trained to cold-call SMB advertisers for a hypothetical OpenAI advertising platform. The rep cannot just memorize a script — they need to understand positioning, qualification, compliance, and judgment.

04 How it works

From SOP to a trained, tested employee — in minutes.

01

Upload SOP

A manager uploads or pastes a process document, Loom transcript, checklist, or training guide.

02

Generate Training

WorkFlow turns the process into role-specific instructions, a quiz, an execution checklist, a QA rubric, and training video scenes.

03

Test Trainee

The trainee completes a quiz or submits a sample work output. In our demo, the trainee submits a cold-call pitch.

04

Evaluate Mistake

WorkFlow checks the response against the SOP and flags issues — overpromising, weak positioning, skipped qualification, compliance risk.

05

Retrain Gap

Instead of repeating the full course, WorkFlow generates a short retraining module targeting the exact gap.
