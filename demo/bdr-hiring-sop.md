# SOP/Training

SOP: How to Qualify and Pitch ChatGPT Ads to SMB Advertisers

Goal:
Train a BPO sales representative to identify, qualify, and pitch SMB advertisers for a new AI advertising product called ChatGPT Ads.

Role:
BPO Sales Development Representative

Target Customer:
Small and midsize businesses that already spend money on digital advertising and want to reach high-intent customers.

Best-Fit SMBs:

- Local service businesses
- Law firms
- Dental offices
- Medspas
- Home services companies
- Auto repair shops
- Real estate brokerages
- Travel and hospitality businesses
- B2B service companies
- eCommerce brands with clear product-market fit

Qualification Criteria:
A qualified SMB advertiser should have:

1. An active business website
2. A clear product or service offering
3. Existing ad spend on Google, Meta, Yelp, TikTok, or local directories
4. A defined customer type
5. A clear conversion goal such as calls, bookings, form fills, purchases, or appointments
6. Enough monthly budget to test a new channel
7. A person responsible for marketing decisions

Disqualification Criteria:
Do not qualify businesses that:

- Do not have a clear offer
- Do not have a working website or landing page
- Cannot explain who their customer is
- Have no ability to handle new leads
- Are asking for guaranteed results
- Are in restricted or high-risk categories
- Are not ready to spend on advertising

Research Steps:

1. Review the company website
2. Identify what the business sells
3. Identify the primary customer
4. Check if they are running ads or have active marketing
5. Look for signs of growth, such as multiple locations, new offers, hiring, or high review volume
6. Identify why ChatGPT Ads could be relevant to their customer acquisition strategy

Outreach Requirements:
The first message must:

1. Reference the business specifically
2. Mention a likely customer acquisition goal
3. Explain ChatGPT Ads in simple terms
4. Avoid hype, vague AI claims, or unrealistic promises
5. Ask for a low-friction next step

Product Explanation:
ChatGPT Ads helps businesses reach people when they are actively exploring products, services, decisions, and recommendations inside ChatGPT. Ads should be clearly labeled and separate from organic answers. The sales rep should explain the opportunity as a new discovery channel, not as a replacement for Google or Meta.

Approved Positioning:

- "This could become a new high-intent discovery channel for SMBs."
- "The goal is to help businesses show up when people are actively researching options."
- "This is best for companies that already understand paid acquisition and want to test emerging channels."
- "This should be treated as an experimental channel with clear tracking and budget discipline."

Do Not Say:

- "This will replace Google Ads."
- "You are guaranteed to get leads."
- "ChatGPT will recommend your business organically."
- "Your ad will influence ChatGPT's answer."
- "This works for every business."
- "You do not need a landing page or tracking."

Discovery Questions:

1. Are you currently running paid ads?
2. What channels are you using today?
3. What is your monthly ad budget?
4. What does a qualified lead or customer look like for you?
5. What action do you want people to take?
6. Who manages marketing decisions?
7. Do you have tracking in place for calls, forms, bookings, or purchases?
8. Are you open to testing a new ad channel if the targeting and reporting are clear?

Good Outreach Example:
Hi Sarah, I noticed your medspa is running promotions around Botox and laser treatments, and it looks like bookings are the main goal. We are speaking with SMB advertisers about ChatGPT Ads, a new way to reach people while they are actively researching services and comparing options. It may be worth testing as an additional discovery channel alongside Google and Meta. Open to a quick conversation?

Bad Outreach Example:
Hi, we help businesses grow with AI advertising. ChatGPT Ads can get you more customers and replace your current marketing. Let me know if you want to learn more.

CRM Logging Requirements:
After every outreach attempt, log:

- Company name
- Contact name
- Industry
- Current ad channels
- Estimated monthly ad budget
- Main conversion goal
- Qualification status
- Objections
- Next follow-up date
- AE escalation status

Escalation Criteria:
Escalate to an AE when the prospect:

- Has existing ad spend
- Has a clear conversion goal
- Understands paid acquisition
- Asks about pricing, targeting, reporting, or launch timelines
- Wants a demo or advertiser onboarding conversation

Quality Standards:
A good BPO sales rep should:

- Understand the product clearly
- Qualify the business before pitching
- Avoid overpromising
- Explain the channel in simple business language
- Ask good discovery questions
- Log accurate CRM notes
- Escalate only qualified prospect
