# BDR Hiring SOP

> Demo input. Replace with your real SOP.

## Goal

Hire a Business Development Representative (BDR).

## Steps

1. Open req in ATS with title, level, and target start date.
2. Source candidates: inbound applicants + outbound LinkedIn outreach.
3. Recruiter screen (30 min) — culture, motivation, baseline comms.
4. Hiring-manager interview (45 min) — role play + objection handling.
5. Panel — sales leader + cross-functional peer.
6. Reference checks: 2 references, prior managers preferred.
7. Offer + close.

## Pass criteria

- Clear written communication.
- Demonstrates resilience under pressure.
- Coachable — accepts and applies feedback in role play.
